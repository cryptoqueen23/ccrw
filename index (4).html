About CCRW

Established 2023

Coryell County Republican Women is a grassroots organization dedicated to advancing and championing Republican ideals and values. We achieve this by collaborating with Republican Party nominees, staying well-informed in politics, and advocating for the efficacy of women in government. CCRW is affiliated with the Texas Federation of Republican Women (TFRW) and the National Federation of Republican Women (NFRW), and is registered with the Texas Ethics Commission.

Formed in March 2023 to fill a void in the county's grassroots movement, CCRW became affiliated with TFRW and received its charter from NFRW in April 2023.

• Increase the effectiveness of women in the cause of good government

• Disseminate information to all members

• Inform the public through political education and activity

• Foster loyalty to the Republican Party at all levels of government

• Promote the principles of the Republican Party

• Work for Republican candidates in all elections, within PAC guidelines

• Support the objectives and policies of the Republican Party

• Get young people involved in the political process

What We Do

Three ways we show up for Coryell County

Advocate

We advocate

We educate members on the issues shaping our county, state, and country, and give conservative women a organized voice in local politics.

Elect

We elect

We recruit, train, and support conservative women running for office and serving on local boards and commissions.

Serve

We serve

From voter registration drives to community service projects, our members put conservative values into action across the county.
